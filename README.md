# Alef Bet Adventures

I want to create a learning app in Hebrew. The app will start from the lowest level of learning the letters and the difference between the letters. For example, it will show the child two letters and he will have to click on the correct letter on the keyboard. He will hear Alef, he will see Alef and Bet, and he will have to click on the letter or on the side, left or right, wherever the letter is. The other option in the app, he will have to read himself and the son sitting next to him will have to confirm that he read it correctly. The whole app will be in Hebrew, so it has to be from right to left. It also includes games that help you learn to read and of course it has to include sound waves that make the correct words audible. A, B, C.
